# HAMS Group website preparation

## Sources and decisions

Reviewed all 21 pages of `img/HAMS_Group_Brand_Book_Human_Progress (1).pdf` and the WhatsApp brand reference image. Logo files are supplied in SVG, PNG, JPEG and AI formats under `img/Logo/`.

Use the PDF as the working authority where the references differ: its primary font is Inter, with Montserrat as a fallback; the image suggests Montserrat. The PDF specifies navy #082D5B; the image labels navy #0B2D5B. Use #082D5B for the website interface and preserve the original logo artwork.

## Purpose and audience

Present HAMS as a diversified Nepalese group united by Human Progress. Serve customers, institutional partners, prospective employees and investors through clear business discovery and credible company information.

Master signature: Moving Life Forward.

Voice: clear, human, confident, responsible and supported by evidence. Publish numerical claims, credentials and outcomes only when verified.

## Visual direction

- Refined editorial layouts, generous whitespace, strong navy headlines, thin rules and restrained gold details.
- Palette: navy #082D5B, gold #D4AF37, cloud white #F7F8FA, deep slate #17212B, cool grey #6B7C8F.
- Approximate balance from the brand book: 70% light surfaces, 20% navy, 10% gold. Check text contrast; use navy for button labels on gold rather than small gold text on white.
- Inter for headings and body, with responsive web sizes rather than copying print point sizes.
- Use supplied SVG logos with intact proportions and clear space. Review primary and secondary versions against their intended backgrounds before implementation.
- Photography: authentic people, care, mobility, teams and Nepalese settings, with natural light. The supplied composite is a reference board, not a production hero photograph.
- Use the arc sparingly. Keep motion subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Proposed site structure

1. Home: purpose, portfolio, evidence, people and next steps.
2. About HAMS: story, mission, vision and five core values.
3. Businesses: portfolio overview and four business detail pages.
4. Leadership: verified biographies, portraits and governance.
5. Impact: substantiated human outcomes and initiatives.
6. Careers: culture, learning, people stories and real openings.
7. News: approved announcements and business milestones.
8. Contact: verified corporate and business contact routes.

Business page sequence follows the PDF: outcome, capability, proof, leadership, contact. Add privacy information and other applicable legal content when the site's data collection is defined.

## Homepage content outline

### Hero

Eyebrow: HAMS GROUP

Headline: Moving Life Forward.

Draft supporting copy: Across healthcare, mobility, medical technology and digital services, we build businesses that help people live healthier, move freely and work smarter.

Primary action: Explore our businesses.

Visual: a licensed or company-owned human or landscape photograph, composed for readable text and mobile cropping.

### One shared purpose

Heading: Many businesses. One human purpose.

Draft copy: We believe progress matters most when people can experience it. HAMS brings capable people, dependable technology and responsible innovation together to improve everyday life.

Link: Discover HAMS.

### Four expressions of progress

| Business | Brand-book message | Content focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hospitals | Better Health. Humanly Delivered. | Care, capability and access |
| Automobiles | Greater Mobility. More Possibility. | Freedom, access and dependable service |
| Medical Devices | Better Tools. Better Decisions. | Clinical tools, diagnostics and institutional capability |
| IT & ITES | Smarter Technology. Stronger Business. | Digital services, connection and business capability |

These are brand architecture labels. Confirm actual legal entities, operating brands, services and destinations before publishing detail pages.

### Progress in practice

Use verified case studies and outcomes. Each story should identify the human need, HAMS contribution and supported result. No invented statistics, testimonials, partnerships or certifications.

### People and culture

Introduce Human First, Earn Trust, Raise the Standard, Build for Tomorrow and One HAMS. Link to leadership and careers when real content is available.

### News and contact

Show genuine dated news if supplied. End with a clear partnership/contact action and verified corporate details.

## Content needed before publication

- Official company description, history and office address.
- Actual subsidiaries, operating brands, service descriptions and approved external links.
- Leadership names, roles, biographies and image permissions.
- Contact email, phone, enquiry routing and social profiles.
- Original photographs with usage rights and suitable crops.
- Evidence for impact stories, scale claims, accreditations and partnerships.
- Approved news and current career opportunities.
- Intended form processing, privacy text, domain and hosting requirements.
